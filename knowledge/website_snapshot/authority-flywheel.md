The method
## What "flywheel" actually means here.
A flywheel is hard to start and easy to keep turning. Content works the same way.
Month one is mostly setup: positioning, research, scripts, the automation. From then on, each month runs on what the previous one taught us. Which hooks held attention? Which topics led to DMs? Which questions kept coming up on calls? Those answers go straight into the next batch of scripts.
Over time you build two things at once: a library of your best thinking that keeps working long after it's posted, and a clear picture of what your buyers actually respond to.
