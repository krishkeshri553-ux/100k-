# How it works, step by step.
Five steps. Only one of them needs you. Here's what happens, who does it and what you get at each stage.
01 Day 1–2 — Onboarding & authority positioning: You complete a structured onboarding form about your offer, your clients and your goals. Your private Discord channel goes live the same day. What you get: Authority Flywheel™ roadmap; Positioning strategy (PDF); Content & angle blueprint; 1:1 Discord channel. Delivered within 48 hours of onboarding.
02 Week 1 — Research & content blueprint: We study your market, your competitors and the conversations your buyers are already having. That research decides the hooks, topics and angles, so scripts aren't built on guesses. What you get: Market research; Content strategy; Hooks & angles
03 Every week — Scripting & recording: We write 30 scripts a month. You record them in about 45–60 minutes a week, on your phone or webcam. That's the only part that needs you. What you get: 30 scripts a month; Weekly recording plan
04 Every week — Editing & distribution: Your raw takes become finished, branded short-form videos, scheduled and published on Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Facebook. What you get: 30 videos a month; 4 platforms; Scheduled publishing
05 Ongoing — Ongoing support & optimisation: We set up ManyChat DM automation, lead qualification and call booking, then track execution every week and refine the content angles every month. What you get: ManyChat DM automation; Lead-qualification flow; Call-booking system; Weekly tracking reports; Monthly angle optimisation

## What we need from you
- An honest onboarding form. The better we understand your offer, the better the scripts.
- About 45–60 minutes a week to record
- Access to your social accounts and a ManyChat account connected to your profile
- Replies in Discord within 48 business hours, so nothing stalls
## What you can stop doing
- Deciding what to post; Writing captions and scripts; Editing, or managing an editor; Remembering to publish on four apps; Replying to every "interested!" comment by hand

## About the process.
Q: How much of my time does this take? A: Plan on 45–60 minutes a week to record from scripts we've already written, plus a few minutes to answer questions in your Discord channel. Your phone or webcam is enough. Onboarding takes a little longer in the first week because we need to understand your offer properly.
Q: What do I need to record with? A: Your phone or a webcam is enough. We send you the scripts and a simple recording plan, and we handle the editing, captions and branding. Good natural light and a quiet room make the biggest difference, and we'll help you set that up during onboarding.
Q: How quickly do things start? A: After you complete the onboarding form, your Authority Flywheel™ roadmap, positioning strategy and content blueprint are delivered within 48 hours, and your private Discord channel is live from day one. We agree the production and publishing calendar with you during onboarding.
