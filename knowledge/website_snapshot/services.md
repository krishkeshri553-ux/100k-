## How the four services hand off to each other.

01 Positioning & strategy — Decide what you want to be known for, and who it's for.
Why it matters: Content without positioning attracts the wrong audience. Ten thousand views from people who will never buy a high-ticket offer are worth less than two hundred from people who might.
What you get: A structured onboarding form and positioning session; Your Authority Flywheel™ roadmap and positioning strategy, delivered within 48 hours of onboarding; A content and angle blueprint: the topics, beliefs and objections your content needs to cover; Market and competitor research before scripting starts

02 Short-form content, scripted for you — 30 scripts a month, written in your voice.
Why it matters: Most experts don't freeze on camera because they lack knowledge. They freeze because they're deciding what to say while recording. Scripts remove that decision, so recording becomes a 45–60 minute routine, not a creative project.
What you get: 30 scripts a month built on your positioning; Hooks and angles drawn from the research, not guesswork; A simple weekly recording plan for your phone or webcam; Monthly angle optimisation based on what performed

03 Editing & distribution — Finished videos, published on four platforms.
Why it matters: Consistency is where most content efforts die. When editing and publishing sit with you, a busy client week means a silent account. When they sit with us, your accounts keep publishing whether or not you had time to think about them.
What you get: 30 short-form videos edited and branded every month; Retention-focused editing: hooks, pacing and on-brand styling; Scheduling and publishing on Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Facebook; A consistent publishing calendar

04 DM automation & inbound system — Turn comments into conversations and booked calls.
Why it matters: Attention fades fast. When someone comments on a video, the best time to reply is right away, and nobody can do that by hand across four platforms. Automation answers instantly, asks the qualifying questions and hands serious buyers a booking link.
What you get: ManyChat comment-to-DM automation; Lead-qualification questions; Call-booking flow; Weekly execution tracking reports
