# Refund Policy
Last updated: 24 October 2026. Questions about this page? Email info@hustlegrowthz.com.
**All payments made to HustleGrowthz Private Limited are non-refundable.** Please read this policy before you buy.
## Monthly plan
Payments already made are not refunded, including for partially used months. You can cancel at any time before your next billing date by emailing info@hustlegrowthz.com. Your service continues until the end of the month you've paid for, and you won't be charged again.
## Annual plan
The annual plan is paid upfront for 12 months at a discounted rate. It is non-refundable, including if you stop using the service before the 12 months end, regardless of whether onboarding has started or how much of the service has been delivered.
## No results guarantee
We do not guarantee specific business outcomes, revenue, leads or follower growth, and refunds are not given on that basis.
## Billing errors
If you believe you were charged incorrectly, for example charged twice, email us within 7 days of the payment and we will investigate and correct any genuine error.
