# Content that wins the listing for you.
Buyers and sellers pick an agent they already feel they know. Before anyone calls, they want proof you know the market, a sense of how you work, and a reason to choose you over the agent on every sign. Short-form video does that, if it's consistent and it's local.
## You close deals. Your content doesn't show it.
Most agents we speak to have the same pattern. Showings, calls and paperwork fill the week. Content gets whatever energy is left, which usually means a listing photo, a "just sold" post, or nothing at all for a fortnight.
The result is an account that looks like every other agent's account. People in your area can't tell what you know that others don't, or why they should call you first. So when they're ready to move, they call whoever they saw most recently.

## Four kinds of content that build trust before the call.
Point of view — What you see in your market that most agents won't say out loud: pricing myths, timing, what really sells a home.
Method — How you work, explained simply: your pricing approach, how you prepare a listing, how you negotiate.
Proof in motion — Real deals (with permission): the situation, what you did and how it closed. No hype, no invented numbers.
Objections — The doubts people raise before choosing an agent, answered in public: commission, timing, "should I wait?"
