Title: Frequently Asked Questions | HustleGrowthz
# Straight answers.
If your question isn't here, email info@hustlegrowthz.com or ask on a strategy call.
Q: What exactly does HustleGrowthz do? A: We run your short-form social media for you. We work out your positioning, research your market, write your scripts, edit the videos, publish them on Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Facebook, and set up the DM automation that turns interested viewers into conversations and booked calls. You do one thing: record. Everything before and after the camera is ours.
Q: How much of my time does this take? A: Plan on 45–60 minutes a week to record from scripts we've already written, plus a few minutes to answer questions in your Discord channel. Your phone or webcam is enough. Onboarding takes a little longer in the first week because we need to understand your offer properly.
Q: Do I need an existing audience? A: No. The system is built to work whether you have 500 followers or 50,000. A bigger audience gives you more people to reach on day one, but it isn't a requirement. What matters more is that you have a clear, proven offer that people already pay for.
Q: What is the difference between the monthly and annual plan? A: Only the billing. Both plans include the full service: 30 videos a month, strategy, scripts, editing, publishing on four platforms and the DM system. Monthly is $999 billed every month. Annual is $5,999 billed once for 12 months. Twelve monthly payments would cost $11,988, so annual saves $5,989, about 50%.
Q: Can I cancel? A: Monthly: yes, at any time before your next billing date. The service continues to the end of the month you've paid for, and you won't be charged again. Annual: it's a 12-month commitment, paid upfront. The full wording is in our terms.
Q: Do you guarantee leads, clients or revenue? A: No, and you should be careful with anyone who does. Results depend on your offer, your price, your market and how consistently you show up. Those things are partly outside our control. What we commit to is the work: the strategy, 30 scripted and edited videos a month, consistent publishing, the DM system and weekly tracking so you can see what's happening.
Q: Who is this not for? A: It isn't a fit if you sell low-ticket products, if you're looking for shortcuts or overnight results, or if you aren't willing to record consistently. It works best for coaches, consultants and experts with a high-ticket offer who know their stuff and need it seen.
Q: What do I need to record with? A: Your phone or a webcam is enough. We send you the scripts and a simple recording plan, and we handle the editing, captions and branding. Good natural light and a quiet room make the biggest difference, and we'll help you set that up during onboarding.
Q: How quickly do things start? A: After you complete the onboarding form, your Authority Flywheel™ roadmap, positioning strategy and content blueprint are delivered within 48 hours, and your private Discord channel is live from day one. We agree the production and publishing calendar with you during onboarding.
Q: Who owns the content? A: You do. Once a payment is made in full, the final videos and scripts produced for that period belong to you.
Q: What happens on the strategy call? A: It's a free 30-minute call. We look at your offer, your current content and where your clients come from today, and we tell you honestly whether the Authority Flywheel™ makes sense for you. If it does, we'll explain how we'd approach your first month. If it doesn't, we'll say so.
Q: Which countries do you work with? A: We work with coaches, consultants and founders in the US, UK, Canada, Australia, Europe and the UAE, and with clients anywhere else in the world. Everything runs remotely through Discord and shared drives, and all prices are in US dollars.
Q: Why not just hire a freelance editor? A: An editor edits. That still leaves you to choose the angles, write the scripts, keep a publishing schedule across four platforms and handle the DMs that come in. Most experts don't stall because the editing is bad. They stall because nobody owns the whole system. We do. If you only need editing, a freelancer may be the better choice, and we'll tell you that on the call.
