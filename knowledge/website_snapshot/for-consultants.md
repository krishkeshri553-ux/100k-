# Let prospects see your judgment before they ever brief you.
Consulting is bought on perceived expertise. A steady stream of short, sharp videos about how you think lets potential clients qualify you, and themselves, long before a proposal.
## Billable work always wins the week.
When you sell your time, every hour spent on content is an hour not billed. So content becomes the thing you'll do "when this project wraps up", and the next project always arrives first.
Meanwhile, buyers are researching. Founders and operators scroll short-form video too, and the consultant they've already watched explain their exact problem has an advantage before the first call.
We take content off your billable calendar. We write 30 scripts a month from your positioning and research, you record them in about 45–60 minutes a week, and we edit and publish everything. ## Show how you think, not what you sell.
The teardown — Take a common decision in your field and walk through how you'd evaluate it. It shows judgment, which is what clients actually buy.
The expensive mistake — A mistake you see businesses make again and again, what it costs and what to do instead.
"What I'd do if…" — Short scenarios that let a prospect imagine you working on their problem.
Contrarian takes — Where you disagree with standard industry advice, and why. Specific beats safe.
