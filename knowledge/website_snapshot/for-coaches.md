# Content that has the first sales conversation for you.
Coaching is bought on trust. Before anyone books a call, they want to know how you think, whether you get their problem and whether you're for real. Short-form video answers all three, if it's consistent and it's on-message.
## Your clients get results. Your content doesn't show it.
Most coaches we speak to have the same pattern. Sessions, calls and client work fill the week. Content gets whatever energy is left, which usually means motivational quotes, a reel copied from a trend, or nothing at all for a fortnight.
The result is an account that looks like every other coach's account. Prospects can't tell what you specifically do, who it's for, or why your programme costs what it costs. So they don't book, or they book and ask for a discount.
The Authority Flywheel™ fixes the input. We decide what you're known for, write 30 scripts a month that teach it, and publish them on four platforms so the right people keep seeing you. It costs $999 a month or $5,999 a year (see pricing), and we work with coaches in the US, UK, Canada, Australia, Europe and the UAE.
## Four kinds of content that build trust before the call.
Point of view — What you believe that most people in your space get wrong. This is what makes someone follow you instead of the next coach.
Method — Pieces of your framework explained simply, so viewers can see how you think and what working with you would feel like.
Proof in motion — Client situations (with permission, or anonymised) that show the before, the shift and the after without hype.
Objections — The doubts people raise before buying coaching, answered in public: price, time, "I've tried this before".
## The DM system, set up for coaching offers.
A video ends with a simple prompt: comment a word to get a resource. ManyChat sends it instantly, then asks two or three questions you'd normally ask on a discovery call, like what they're working on and what they've tried.
People who fit get your booking link. People who don't still get something useful, and you stop spending evenings answering "info?" by hand. We set it up and track it weekly, so you can see which topics start real conversations.
